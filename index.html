The Traditions

Rituals, Explained

A little guide to the customs you'll see us celebrate.

Mehndi

Intricate henna patterns are painted onto the bride's hands and feet, believed to bring beauty, joy and good luck into married life.

Haldi

Turmeric paste is applied to the couple as a blessing for glowing skin and to ward off evil before the wedding.

Mandap Muhurat

The sacred wedding canopy is consecrated at an astrologically auspicious moment, marking the true start of the ceremony.

The Pheras

The couple take seven sacred rounds around the holy fire, each step a vow for the life they'll build together.
